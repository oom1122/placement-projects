National Tiger

The tiger is a large, powerful carnivorous mammal known for its striking orange coat with black stripes. It is the largest member of the cat family and lives mainly in parts of Asia, including India, Russia, and Southeast Asia.

National Banyan Tree

The banyan tree is a large and majestic tree known for its unique way of growing. It has thick branches that spread out widely, and from these branches, aerial roots hang down and grow into the ground to form new trunks. Over time, this allows the tree to cover a vast area, making it look like a forest by itself. The banyan tree is often considered a symbol of strength, stability, and shelter. In many cultures, it holds religious and cultural importance, and people often gather under its shade for rest or community meetings.

National Bird

The Indian peacock is the national bird of India. It was declared the national bird in 1963 because of its rich religious and cultural importance in Indian traditions. The peacock is known for its beautiful, colorful feathers, especially the male, which spreads its large, fan-shaped tail during the monsoon season in a dance to attract a mate. The bird symbolizes grace, beauty, and elegance. It is also considered sacred in Indian mythology, often associated with gods like Lord Krishna and Saraswati.
